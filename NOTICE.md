This repository is prepared as an educational website source repository. It may reference or contain third-party names, trademarks, documents, standards, product materials, screenshots, logos, and generated reference data.

## General Rules

- Third-party trademarks and product names belong to their respective owners.
- Third-party documents keep their original copyright and license terms.
- If a document is publicly available from an authoritative source, prefer linking to that source instead of redistributing a copy when rights are unclear.
- If a file cannot be confidently redistributed, keep it out of the public Git history.

## Known Review Areas

Before the repository is made public, review these areas carefully:

- Astra Linux documentation and PDF materials;
- vendor and course handouts in PDF/PPTX/DOCX format;
- materials of the programme «Образовательная лаборатория Kaspersky Academy | Безопасность приложений» (АО «Лаборатория Касперского»), except for the first-day files explicitly published in `appsec-lections/downloads/day-01/` on the site owner's instruction;
- logos in `is/logo` and course-branded images;
- SPDX-derived website/data under `spdx`;
- any copied screenshots or generated snapshots from previously published pages.

## SPDX-Derived Material

The `spdx/` tree is an unofficial educational mirror/derivative of SPDX License
List pages and data, with additional Russian-language notes on some pages. It
is not endorsed by the SPDX Workgroup or The Linux Foundation. Original SPDX
notices and original license texts retain their own terms; author-created
Russian notes are covered by the content license unless a page states
otherwise.

## Excluded Pending Review

The repository intentionally excludes course-branded photographs, vendor
handouts, distributable office/PDF archives, and Astra Linux material mirrors
unless redistribution rights are explicit. Prefer linking to authoritative
sources instead of storing copies when rights are unclear.

The `appsec-lections/` site contains author-created teaching notes and
original illustrations, plus the explicitly authorized original first-day
archive in `appsec-lections/downloads/day-01/` (slide photographs,
transcripts, participant materials and laboratory results). It is not an
official Kaspersky resource. The external name is used for factual attribution
and does not imply endorsement.

(https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I5h4Bz99Ex0). It is included unchanged, with
attribution; all rights belong to its author, and the author has not stated a
license. It will be removed at the request of the rights holder.

The presentations and notes on `kamaz-ai/video-notes.html`
(`kamaz-ai/materials/video-notes/agent-84-deck.html`, `rag-deck.html`) were
prepared for educational purposes from two videos of the same channel:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I5h4Bz99Ex0 and
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIrubiKm480. They retell the videos in the
course author's own words, contain no frames from the videos, and link to the
originals. Rights to the videos belong to the channel's author.
